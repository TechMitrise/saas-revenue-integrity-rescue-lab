# Growth Engine

## Positioning

Primary promise:

> Find the leak. Rescue the revenue. Modernize the cause.

Legacy Laravel promise:

> Modernize legacy Laravel without a risky rewrite.

The service combines business-risk diagnosis, immediate rescue, incremental modernization, and evidence-backed verification.

## Ideal Customer Profile

Prioritize B2B SaaS companies that have:

- A business-critical Laravel application
- An old Laravel or PHP version
- Custom subscription or billing workflows
- Limited automated test coverage
- Manual or fragile deployments
- Recent pricing, packaging, billing, or platform migrations
- Unexplained reconciliation issues or customer billing complaints
- Pressure to modernize without funding a rewrite

Primary buyers and champions:

| Persona | Primary concern |
| --- | --- |
| Founder or CEO | Growth, risk, and avoiding a costly rewrite |
| CFO or Finance leader | Revenue leakage, reconciliation, and close |
| CTO or VP Engineering | Framework risk, reliability, and delivery speed |
| Revenue Operations | Detection, prioritization, and ownership |
| Engineering manager | Tests, architecture, upgrades, and deployment safety |

## Offer Ladder

| Stage | Offer | Deliverable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Laravel Rescue Review | Short discovery and qualification call |
| 2 | Legacy Laravel Rescue Assessment | Evidence, risks, roadmap, and estimates |
| 3 | Rescue Sprint | Urgent business, security, reliability, and deployment fixes |
| 4 | Modernization Program | Incremental framework and architecture modernization |
| 5 | Reliability Partnership | Ongoing upgrades, tests, monitoring, and improvements |

## Acquisition Loop

```text
Qualified Laravel account
  -> Rescue checklist or targeted insight
  -> Personalized short video
  -> Rescue Review call
  -> Paid assessment
  -> Rescue Sprint
  -> Modernization engagement
  -> Verified case study
  -> Reusable content and referrals
```

## Outreach Message

> We help SaaS teams identify business and technical risks hidden inside legacy Laravel applications. Our Legacy Laravel Rescue process creates a safety net around critical workflows, rescues urgent failures, and delivers an incremental modernization plan without forcing a rewrite. Would a short Laravel Rescue Review be useful for your team?

## Discovery Questions

- Which Laravel and PHP versions are in production?
- Which workflows create the most business or operational risk?
- What prevents the team from upgrading?
- How are billing, payments, subscriptions, or entitlements implemented?
- What automated test coverage exists around critical behavior?
- How are deployments, rollbacks, queues, and scheduled jobs managed?
- Which incidents or customer complaints have repeated?
- What business deadline makes modernization important now?

## Growth Guardrails

- Sell the assessment and outcome before expanding the platform.
- Do not invent customer results or case studies.
- Label representative demonstrations accurately.
- Avoid broad "we modernize everything" positioning.
- Use prospect objections and assessment findings to decide which product capabilities to build next.
