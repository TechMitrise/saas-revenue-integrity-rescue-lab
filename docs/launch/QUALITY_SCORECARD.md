## First review and focused corrections

The first rendered review identified four weaknesses:

1. **Buyer clarity:** the proof hero led with an abstract revenue-leak statement instead of the locked commercial story.
2. **Visual consistency:** all six initial captures contained transient toasts.
3. **Evidence integrity:** the comparison was captured after reconciliation, so fragile access appeared active.
4. **CTA clarity:** the page prioritised running the lab rather than requesting the approved Risk Triage.

Focused corrections:

- changed the hero to "Payment succeeded. Access failed.";
- made **Request a Legacy SaaS Risk Triage** the single primary CTA;
- added the 107-second walkthrough, six-frame gallery, buyer fit and USD 1,500-3,000 Audit;
- hid transient capture UI and reset before the final comparison;
- re-rendered and visually inspected desktop, mobile and all six application frames;
- kept every client URL, testimonial and production outcome as a placeholder or explicit exclusion.
